# Private Health Trust provider directory

Reviewed: 2026-09-18

Editorial shortlist; not a ranking by clinical outcomes. Descriptions summarize the linked sources. Caron campus and program entries overlap.

## Featured treatment programs

### Plugged In Recovery

Scottsdale, Arizona · Residential

A small residential addiction program combining individual and group counseling, recovery support, and experiential activities in Scottsdale.

[Source](https://recovery.com/plugged-in-recovery-scottsdale-arizona/)

### J. Flowers Health Institute

Houston, Texas · Diagnostic evaluation & individualized treatment

A concierge program centered on comprehensive diagnostic evaluation, stabilization, and individualized care for complex mental health, substance use, and medical concerns.

[Source](https://recovery.com/j-flowers-health-institute-texas/)

### Bayside Marin

San Rafael, California · Residential

Residential addiction and mental health care in Marin County, with individual and group therapy, family work, and a phased approach to discharge planning.

[Source](https://recovery.com/bayside-marin/)

### Carrara Treatment Malibu

Malibu, California · Detox & residential

A private Malibu setting offering addiction treatment, detoxification, individual therapy, psychiatric care, and wellness services.

[Source](https://recovery.com/carrara-treatment-malibu-california/)

### All Points North Lodge

Vail Valley, Colorado · Residential

A Colorado residential campus offering addiction, mental health, and trauma-focused care, with programming for professionals, veterans, and athletes.

[Source](https://recovery.com/all-points-north/)

### The Beach Cottage at Seasons in Malibu

Malibu, California · Residential mental health

A residential mental health program offering individualized therapy in a coastal setting. This specific Seasons program is focused on mental health.

[Source](https://recovery.com/residential/the-beach-cottage-seasons-in-malibu-california/)

### Borden Cottage

Camden, Maine · Residential

A small residential program for substance use and co-occurring conditions, with individualized therapy, neuropsychological assessment, and continuing-care planning.

[Source](https://recovery.com/borden-cottage/)

### Peak Path Health

Los Angeles, California · Residential

A small residential addiction program serving clients with co-occurring mental health needs, with individual therapy and support for the transition to outpatient care.

[Source](https://recovery.com/residential/peak-path-health-los-angeles/)

### The Guest House Ocala

Silver Springs, Florida · Residential

An adult treatment center near Ocala focused on trauma and related substance use, process addictions, anxiety, and depression.

[Source](https://www.theguesthouseocala.com/)

### Alta Mira Recovery Programs

Sausalito, California · Residential

Residential treatment for substance use and co-occurring mental health disorders, with psychological assessment, medical services, family programming, and continuing care.

[Source](https://www.altamirarecovery.com/)

### Caron Pennsylvania

Wernersville, Pennsylvania · Residential campus

Caron’s Pennsylvania campus offers residential addiction treatment and specialized tracks, alongside medical detox and continuing-care services.

[Source](https://www.caron.org/locations/caron-pennsylvania)

### Caron Grand View

Wernersville, Pennsylvania · Residential specialty program

Caron’s Pennsylvania residential program designed around the needs of executives and other professionals seeking privacy and individualized care.

[Source](https://www.caron.org/treatment-programs/executives)

### Caron Florida Adult Residential

Delray Beach, Florida · Residential

An adult residential program for substance use and co-occurring disorders, with family education and a personalized continuing-care plan.

[Source](https://www.caron.org/treatment-programs/adult-residential-program)

### Caron Ocean Drive

Florida · Residential specialty program

A Caron Florida program providing discreet, individualized addiction care for affluent adults and their families.

[Source](https://www.caron.org/treatment-programs/ocean-drive-florida)

### Caron Renaissance

Boca Raton, Florida · Residential

Caron’s Florida residential program addressing addiction and underlying issues, with an emphasis on sustained change and family involvement.

[Source](https://www.caron.org/treatment-programs/renaissance)
