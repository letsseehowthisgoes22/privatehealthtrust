Curated Provider Directory

Treatment Programs, Recovery Support, and Allied Professionals

The following directory identifies providers across the continuum of care for individuals and families navigating behavioral health challenges. Inclusion reflects editorial assessment of relevance and quality; it does not constitute endorsement. Families should conduct independent evaluation of any provider before engaging their services.

Residential Treatment Programs

Domestic

The Guest House Ocala (Silver Springs, FL) — Trauma-informed residential treatment on a private 52-acre estate. Joint Commission accredited. Specializes in trauma resolution, co-occurring disorders, and the intersection of emotional trauma and addiction. $48,000-$96,000/month.

The Meadows (Wickenburg, AZ) — Pioneer in trauma-focused addiction treatment using a proprietary developmental immaturity model. Joint Commission accredited. Offers specialized tracks for professionals, first responders, and individuals with sex and relationship addiction. $44,000/45 days.

Caron Treatment Centers (Wernersville, PA) — One of the nation's largest nonprofit treatment providers. CARF accredited. Executive program accommodates ongoing professional obligations. NFL partnership for player treatment. Multiple locations.

Hazelden Betty Ford Foundation (Center City, MN, and multiple locations) — The nation's largest nonprofit addiction treatment provider, formed by the 2014 merger of the Hazelden Foundation and the Betty Ford Center. Joint Commission accredited. Offers specialized programs for professionals, young adults, and families.

Sierra Tucson (Tucson, AZ) — Integrative treatment model combining evidence-based therapies with complementary approaches including neurofeedback and equine-assisted therapy. Joint Commission accredited.

The Dunes East Hampton (East Hampton, NY) — Luxury residential treatment on the eastern end of Long Island. Small census with individualized programming. Appeal for patients in the New York metropolitan area who prefer geographic proximity.

International

Paracelsus Recovery (Zurich, Switzerland) — One-client-at-a-time model with a dedicated clinical team for each patient. Comprehensive biochemical restoration, including detailed laboratory analysis and targeted nutritional supplementation. Approximately CHF 95,000-120,000/week.

The Kusnacht Practice (Zurich, Switzerland) — BIO-R biochemical restoration combined with traditional psychotherapeutic approaches. Joint Commission International accredited. Private residence treatment model. From CHF 117,300/week.

The Priory Group (London, UK, and multiple locations) — The UK's leading independent provider of mental health treatment. CQC regulated. Network of hospitals and wellbeing centres across the UK. Programs for addiction, depression, anxiety, and eating disorders.

In-Home Treatment Providers

ALYST Health (Los Angeles, CA) — The first at-home addiction treatment program to receive Joint Commission accreditation. Brings a full clinical team to the patient's residence for intensive treatment while maintaining daily functioning.

Recovery Support Services

SilverBell Global — International network of recovery professionals providing sober companion and recovery coaching services. Operates in major cities worldwide with centralized clinical oversight.

Interactive Health Companies: Interactive Youth Transport · Coast Health Consulting — Sober companion placement, safe transport, and care coordination for UHNW families. Provides credentialed recovery professionals for post-treatment support and monitoring.

Spearhead Health (Beverly Hills, CA) — Concierge-level care management and recovery coaching. Combines clinical case management with lifestyle integration support.

The Advocacy Group (TAG) (Los Angeles, CA) — Comprehensive case management and recovery companionship led by Lauren Brock. Specializes in entertainment industry and UHNW clientele.

Hired Power (Southern California) — Family-owned firm with over two decades of experience in recovery assistance, safe transport, and sober companion services.

Care Management

O'Connor Professional Group — Clinical care management for UHNW families navigating complex behavioral health situations. Serves as an intermediary between families, treatment providers, and professional advisors. National scope.

Concierge Care Collective — Comprehensive in-home care infrastructure including nursing, psychiatric stabilization, and long-term recovery planning. Markets served include New York, Palm Beach, Beverly Hills, and Aspen.

Professional Organizations

SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357 — Free, confidential, 24/7 referral and information service for individuals and families facing substance use disorders and mental health conditions.

NIDA (National Institute on Drug Abuse) — Federal research institute providing evidence-based information on drug use, its consequences, and treatment.

ASAM (American Society of Addiction Medicine) — Professional society for addiction medicine physicians. Publisher of the ASAM Criteria and the Journal of Addiction Medicine.
