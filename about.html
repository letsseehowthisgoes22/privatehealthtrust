About This Publication

Editorial Mission

The Private Client exists to provide trustworthy, evidence-based information to individuals and families navigating behavioral health challenges in the context of significant wealth, public profile, or professional responsibility. Our content is written with the understanding that our readers are intelligent, private, and discerning — and that they deserve information resources that match those qualities.

What We Offer

Our articles cover the clinical, legal, logistical, and personal dimensions of behavioral health — from understanding treatment options and protecting privacy to supporting family recovery and managing the intersection of wellness and wealth. We draw on published research, clinical evidence, and the expertise of professionals who specialize in serving private clients to produce content that is genuinely useful, factually accurate, and respectful of our readers' intelligence and autonomy.

Editorial Independence

This publication is editorially independent. We do not accept payment for coverage or inclusion in our provider directory. When we reference specific programs, providers, or organizations, we do so based on editorial assessment of their relevance and quality. Our recommendations reflect current clinical evidence and professional practice standards.

What We Are Not

We are not a treatment referral service, a marketing platform, or a substitute for professional clinical advice. We do not provide diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or clinical guidance for specific situations. Individuals and families facing behavioral health challenges should consult with qualified clinical professionals who can evaluate their specific circumstances.

Contact

Readers with editorial inquiries, content suggestions, or corrections may reach us at editor@privatehealthtrust.com.
