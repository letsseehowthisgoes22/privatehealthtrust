PARENTS ASK

Questions worth answering clearly.

How do I find the right rehab for my adult child?

Begin with an assessment by a qualified clinician, then compare programs that can treat the identified substance use, mental health, and medical needs. Ask about staff qualifications, family involvement, costs, and the plan for care after discharge.

Who can help manage my son’s or daughter’s mental health care?

A private behavioral health case manager can coordinate appointments, communicate with providers, organize treatment transitions, and support family planning. Ask who will manage the case, what qualifications they hold, and which services are included.
